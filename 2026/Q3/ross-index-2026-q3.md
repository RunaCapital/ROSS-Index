# Top trending open-source startups in Q3 2026

1. 🇺🇸 **[OpenDesign](https://open-design.ai) ([nexu-io/open-design](https://github.com/nexu-io/open-design) 98.7K stars, 81.7x growth)** builds <a href="https://open-design.ai/">Open Design</a>, a desktop AI agent that prototypes websites, mobile apps, and slides. Founded in 2024 in San Jose, CA. Raised an undisclosed amount from GSR, Hillhouse, MindWorks, ClassIn.
2. 🇺🇸 **[Graphify](https://graphify.com) ([Graphify-Labs/graphify](https://github.com/Graphify-Labs/graphify) 122.5K stars, 78.2x growth)** turns codebases, docs, and diagrams into queryable knowledge graphs for AI coding assistants. Founded in 2026 in San Francisco. Raised $0.5M from Y Combinator.
